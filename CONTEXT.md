# Resume

The resume presents the candidate's background through four canonical section types shared by the English and Korean pages.

## Language

**Education**:
Formal academic study completed through a school or university.

**Experience**:
Paid professional employment performed for an organization.
_Avoid_: Career, Work Experience

**Projects**:
Time-bounded work organized around producing a concrete technical or analytical outcome.
_Avoid_: Project

**Activities**:
Clubs, communities, training programs, and organized activities outside paid employment and formal academic study.
_Avoid_: Extracurricular Activities
